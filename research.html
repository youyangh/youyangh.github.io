Selected industry research

Notion2023 – present

Research lead for Notion AI

I've been the research lead across several major Notion AI launches, from AI Q&A and the AI Assistant to AI Connectors, Research Mode, and Custom Agents. I also built the shared infrastructure (an AI research roadmap, insights hub, and recurring sharing rituals) so that findings from many researchers add up to one product story.

AI Assistant alpha

Assumptions workshops, workflow research, and a multi-week alpha study. The findings fed launch readiness and positioning, and contributed to delaying the launch until quality met a higher bar.

Universal Search and AI Connectors

Surveys of third-party tools and workflows, plus alpha and enterprise interviews. This work set connector priorities, setup improvements, and how search results surface across tools.

Custom Agents beta and growth

Beta interviews, in-product surveys, weekly synthesis, and CSAT benchmarks. I traced why active AI users didn't adopt agents: setup, perceived value, cost clarity, reliability, and habit.

Agent permissions and governance

I synthesized enterprise security and admin concerns early and brought Product, Security, Legal, and GTM together around admin controls for agents.

AI insight systems

I built the AI research roadmap, insights hub, and recurring sharing rituals so findings from many researchers and teams ladder into one product narrative.

Notion2023

Core product: Home, Sidebar, Inbox, Sharing

Card sorting, concept and prototype testing, and a large-scale notifications survey. This research shaped the scope of the Home MVP, the Inbox 2.0 strategy, and a company-wide redesign of the permission model.

Lyft2022

Express Drive and EV electrification

Strategic research on driver and renter experience for Express Drive and Flexdrive. I also led mixed-methods research (literature review, interviews, a survey, and focus groups) that informed company-wide EV roadmaps.

Google · Flutter2019 – 2022

Flutter developer journeys and DevTools

A foundational study of Flutter developers' critical user journeys, using interviews, a MaxDiff survey, workshops, and product metrics. It set product priorities and the strategy for Flutter DevTools.

Google · Cloud2017 – 2019

cloud.google.com and 0→1 developer tools

Generative and evaluative research for the redesigned Google Cloud website, plus research ownership for new products, including the Cloud Java experience and Cloud Code.

Facebook2014, 2015
