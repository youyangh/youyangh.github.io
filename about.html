About

I'm a mixed-methods user researcher, most passionate about complex and ambiguous product and business problems. That's why I love building AI products with cross-functional partners: figuring out what an AI feature should do, who it's for, where people find value, and what stops them from trusting it.

I was a founding member of Notion's user research team, and now I manage researchers across Notion AI: Core AI, AI Workflows, Dev Platform, AI Eval, AI Meeting Notes, and AI Governance. Before that I was a Senior UX Researcher at Lyft and worked on Flutter and Google Cloud at Google.

I hold a Ph.D. in Information Science (HCI) from the University of Michigan, an M.A. in Cognition and Cognitive Neuroscience from Michigan State University, and a B.S. in Psychology from Peking University. At Michigan I also taught HCI and information science courses as a graduate student instructor.
