About

I'm a mixed-methods researcher. I'm most useful when a product area is new or ambiguous: figuring out what an AI feature should do, who it's for, where people find value, and what stops them from trusting it.

I was a founding member of Notion's user research team, and now I manage researchers across Notion AI, Custom Agents, and Enterprise. Before that I was a Senior UX Researcher at Lyft and worked on Flutter and Google Cloud at Google.

I hold a Ph.D. in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Michigan School of Information and a B.S. in Psychology from Peking University.

Methods
Interviews, diary studies, large-scale surveys, MaxDiff, concept and usability testing, card sorting, workshops, UXR × DS analysis
Focus areas
AI agents and assistants, knowledge and search, AI growth and adoption, permissions and governance, developer tools
